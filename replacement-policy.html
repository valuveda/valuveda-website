Replacement Policy

ValuVeda Wellness

Our replacement policy explains when a product issue may qualify for replacement and how customers should report a damaged, opened or defective product.

When Replacement May Be Requested

A replacement request may be considered when the product delivered to the customer is received damaged, opened/tampered with, or has a relevant manufacturing/product defect.

Report the Issue Within 24–48 Hours

Customers should contact ValuVeda Wellness as soon as possible and preferably within 24–48 hours of delivery when they notice damage, tampering, an opened package or a relevant product issue. Early reporting helps us investigate the matter efficiently.

Photo or Video Evidence

For a damage, tampering or product-condition complaint, customers may be asked to share clear photographs or video showing the outer package, shipping label, product container and the issue. An unboxing video is strongly recommended because it can help establish the condition of the parcel when opened.

How to Submit a Request

The customer can contact us through the available customer-support number/WhatsApp or email and provide the order details, delivery date and clear evidence of the reported issue. We may ask for additional information that is reasonably required to verify the claim.

Verification of the Complaint

Every replacement request may be reviewed before approval. We may compare the submitted photographs, videos, order details and product/batch information with the circumstances reported by the customer.

Approved Replacement

If the request is found eligible, we may arrange a replacement of the affected product or provide another appropriate resolution depending on the circumstances and applicable consumer rights.

Situations Normally Not Covered

A replacement request is generally not intended for a simple change of mind, a product that is no longer wanted, or damage caused after delivery by improper handling, storage or use. This does not limit any consumer right that cannot legally be excluded.

Product Storage After Delivery

Customers should keep the product properly closed and store it according to the instructions printed on the product packaging. Improper storage after delivery may affect product quality and may not qualify as a delivery-damage claim.

Refunds Where Applicable

If a refund is determined to be applicable, it will be processed through the relevant payment/refund method. The time taken for the amount to appear in the customer's account may depend on the bank, card issuer, payment gateway or other payment service provider.
