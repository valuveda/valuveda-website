# ValuVeda Premium Product UX Specification

## Goal

Create an original premium long-form Ayurvedic wellness shopping experience inspired by the information depth and conversion flow of high-quality D2C product pages, without copying another site's branding, text, imagery or code.

## Experience

1. Premium sticky header with ValuVeda logo and navigation.
2. Hero: authentic product pack, product name, concise wellness positioning, price, trust badges and primary purchase actions.
3. Trust strip: 200g, 15+ herbs, No Added Sugar where label confirms, GMP/FSSAI/lab-tested only where documentation confirms.
4. Story section explaining formulation philosophy without disease-cure promises.
5. Ingredient explorer with botanical names and concise traditional descriptions.
6. Quality section explaining manufacturing and responsible claims.
7. How-to-use section based strictly on the approved label.
8. Product detail accordions: ingredients, quantity, shipping, replacement, storage and relevant warnings.
9. FAQ section.
10. Final conversion CTA.
11. Mobile sticky buy bar.

## Visual direction

- Premium natural palette and typography.
- Botanical motifs and restrained motion.
- Product packaging remains the hero asset.
- Generous whitespace and editorial-style sections.
- Subtle scroll/hover animations, never distracting.
- Fast-loading images, lazy loading below the fold and responsive sizing.
- Accessible contrast, focus states, semantic headings and keyboard-friendly accordions.

## Content rules

- Use only verified ValuVeda product facts.
- Do not invent reviews, certificates, ingredients, dosage, clinical results or medical claims.
- Do not state that the product cures, reverses or guarantees control of a disease.
- Approved product label is the source of truth for dosage and ingredient spelling.

## Purchase paths

Primary: Buy Now / checkout when ecommerce backend is available.
Secondary: Buy on Amazon.
Secondary: WhatsApp assistance/order flow.

The same product should use a single canonical product identity across all channels.

## Mobile

The experience must be designed mobile-first. Primary CTA remains reachable without excessive scrolling. Tables should collapse into cards and long sections should remain readable.
