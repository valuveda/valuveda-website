Shipping Policy

ValuVeda Wellness

Our shipping policy explains standard delivery, processing, delivery timelines and what customers can expect after placing an order with ValuVeda Wellness.

Free Pan-India Shipping

ValuVeda Wellness provides standard shipping across India, subject to courier serviceability at the customer's delivery location. Where free standard shipping is shown for an order, no separate standard delivery charge is intended to be collected from the customer.

Order Processing

After an order is received, the order details are checked and the product is prepared for dispatch. We make reasonable efforts to process and dispatch orders as promptly as possible.

Expected Delivery Timeline

Normal delivery is expected within approximately 4–7 working days after dispatch, depending on the destination and courier service. The displayed timeline is an estimate and not an unconditional guarantee of a particular delivery date.

Weather & Operational Delays

Delivery may sometimes take longer because of heavy rain, extreme weather, floods, natural events, transport disruption, courier operational issues, public holidays, remote-area service limitations or other circumstances outside our reasonable control. In such exceptional situations, delivery may take around 10 days or longer.

Delivery Address

Customers should provide a complete and accurate delivery address, including the correct PIN code and contact number. An incorrect or incomplete address can cause delay, failed delivery or additional delivery attempts.

Tracking & Delivery Updates

Where tracking information is available, the customer may receive or be provided with the relevant tracking details. Courier delivery scans and estimated dates are controlled by the delivery service and may change during transit.

Customer Availability

Customers should remain available at the delivery address or arrange for an authorised person to receive the parcel. If delivery cannot be completed because nobody is available or the address is incorrect, the courier may make another attempt according to its process.

Delivery to Remote or Restricted Locations

Some remote, restricted or otherwise non-serviceable locations may have different delivery timelines or may not be serviceable by a particular courier. We will make reasonable efforts to provide an alternative where possible.

Support for Delivery Issues

If an order appears delayed or there is a delivery problem, customers can contact us with their order details and registered mobile number so that we can assist with the available information.
