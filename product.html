Jamun (Syzygium cumini), Neem (Azadirachta indica), Tulsi (Ocimum tenuiflorum), Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera), Bael (Aegle marmelos), Vijaysar (Pterocarpus marsupium), Shatavari (Asparagus racemosus), Methi (Trigonella foenum-graecum), Saunf (Foeniculum vulgare), Gudmar (Gymnema sylvestre), Harsingar (Nyctanthes arbor-tristis), Karela (Momordica charantia L.), Manjistha (Rubia cordifolia), Chirata (Swertia), Dalchini (Cinnamomum verum).
